Privacy Policy

We believe privacy should be clear. This page explains what information we collect when you contact Atharvax and how we use it.

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect the contact and project information you provide, including your name, business email address, organisation, and project details. We collect only what is needed to respond to your request and provide our services.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, assess potential engagements, communicate about our services, and improve the quality and security of our website. We do not sell personal information.

How information is shared

Information is shared only with people and service providers who need it to operate our website or respond to your request. Those parties are expected to protect the information and use it only for the relevant purpose.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. We retain information for only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, legal obligations, or legitimate business records.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with an enquiry by contacting us. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.
